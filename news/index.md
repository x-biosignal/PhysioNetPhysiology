# Changelog

## PhysioNetPhysiology 0.1.0

- Initial release: Network Physiology for the Physio ecosystem.
- [`timeDelayStability()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/timeDelayStability.md)
  — the core Time Delay Stability (TDS) engine over a multivariate
  physiological series (Bashan et al. 2012).
- [`tdsSurrogateThreshold()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/tdsSurrogateThreshold.md)
  — phase-randomised surrogate significance threshold for TDS links.
- [`tdsNetwork()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/tdsNetwork.md)
  — thresholded, weighted organ-interaction networks.
- [`tdsNetworkByState()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/tdsNetworkByState.md)
  /
  [`tdsReconfiguration()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/tdsReconfiguration.md)
  — state-resolved networks and their reconfiguration across
  physiological states (e.g. sleep stages).
- [`physioNodeMatrix()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/physioNodeMatrix.md)
  — assemble a node matrix of physiological systems.
- Visualisation:
  [`plotDelayStability()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/plotDelayStability.md),
  [`plotTDSnetwork()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/plotTDSnetwork.md),
  [`plotTDSreconfiguration()`](https://x-biosignal.github.io/PhysioNetPhysiology/reference/plotTDSreconfiguration.md).
